Optimal Investment with Switching Preferences

Yu-Jui Huang, Liviu Ignat, Traian A. Pirvu, and Reihaneh Vafadar

Preprint · arXiv:2607.22536.

A PDE and stochastic-control treatment of investment when a future event may increase an investor’s risk aversion. The analysis constructs the value function through nonlinear duality and verifies an optimal strategy.
