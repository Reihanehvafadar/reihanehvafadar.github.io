About me

Academic background↗

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at McMaster University, working with Traian A. Pîrvu and Eric T. Sawyer. My research connects partial differential equations, stochastic analysis, and mathematical finance.

I completed my PhD in Mathematics at Université Laval in 2025, under the supervision of Damir Kinzebulatov. My doctoral work concerned diffusions with singular and distributional drifts. I also teach Introduction to Real Analysis at McMaster.
